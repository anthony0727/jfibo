# AI-evaluation cases (non-production, non-ontology)

These cases are not part of the J-FIBO ontology or its production benchmark.
They explore how a consumer (e.g. an AI agent) would mark, refuse, or
caveat claims that lie outside the auditable evidence boundary of a
Japanese filing.

The ontology itself defines reporting concepts, holder roles, and
institutional relationships — it does not prescribe consumer reasoning.
